## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing student satisfaction through end-of-module surveys across the different schools and training programs within the C&D Group.

The objective is to monitor students' feedback on individual modules, assess teaching quality and learning resources, and identify areas for improvement through interactive Power BI dashboards.

The reporting solution provides a consolidated view of survey response rates, satisfaction levels, module-level performance, and qualitative student feedback.

## Key Features

- **Global Survey Overview:** Monitoring of survey participation, total responses, notifications sent, and overall response rate.
- **Overall Module Satisfaction:** Analysis of satisfaction levels across the different survey questions and educational dimensions.
- **Detailed Satisfaction by Module:** Comparison of satisfaction results by module code and training module.
- **Sentiment Analysis:** Distribution of student feedback across four satisfaction categories: Little Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Unsatisfied, and Very Satisfied.
- **Module Performance Analysis:** Identification of modules with higher or lower satisfaction levels across the different sentiment categories.
- **Comments & Remarks:** Analysis of qualitative student feedback and additional comments to identify improvement opportunities.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Exploration of results by division, school brand, region, academic year, and module.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboards include the following main indicators:

| KPI | Description | Reported Value |
|---|---|---:|
| **Global Response Rate** | Percentage of invited students who submitted a survey response. | 61.89% |
| **Total Responses** | Total number of survey responses received. | 137,063 |
| **Total Notifications** | Total number of survey notifications sent. | 221,449 |

## Analysis Dimensions

The reports provide interactive filtering and analysis across the following dimensions:

- **Division:** BD, C&C, M&E, Num., and OMD.
- **School Brand:** 3A, EPSI, ESAIL, IBS, ICL, IEFT, IET, IFAG, IGEFI, IHEDREA, ILERI, SDC, and WIS.
- **Region:** Amiens, Angers, Arras, Bordeaux, Grenoble, Lille, Lyon, Montpellier, Nantes, National, Nice, Paris, Rennes, and Toulouse.
- **Academic Year:** 2022–2023 and 2023–2024.
- **Module:** Analysis by module code and module name.

## Evaluation Dimensions

The satisfaction surveys cover several aspects of the learning experience, including:

- Quality and relevance of the learning materials.
- Availability and quality of learning resources.
- Teaching quality and instructional effectiveness.
- Module assessment methods.
- Suitability of distance learning arrangements.
- Relevance and quality of module content.
- Balance between theoretical and practical learning.
- Feedback and corrections provided to students.

## Satisfaction Categories

Student responses are analyzed using four sentiment categories:

- **Very Satisfied**
- **Somewhat Satisfied**
- **Little Satisfied**
- **Unsatisfied**

These categories support the comparison of satisfaction levels across survey questions, modules, school brands, and regions.
